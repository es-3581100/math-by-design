A progression can change voice without losing direction.

Begin broad and emphatic. Then tighten the measure. Let the next piece shift sideways, reduce in scale, or switch type family. The movement comes from composition.

“Large → narrow → offset → precise” is often enough to make the eye travel. No spiral-shaped paragraph required.

01 / MASS

Give the primary message about 1.618× the visual authority of its supporting region through width, scale, contrast or whitespace.

02 / FLOW

Handoffs occur at shared edges: headline to explanation, explanation to proof, proof to decision. The dev nodes make those transitions explicit.

03 / VARIATION

A page built from one ratio should not look like one repeated module. Change alignment, type voice, line length and density while keeping the same underlying progression.

04 / MOBILE

On small screens, hierarchy outranks geometry. Preserve order and proportion cues, but allow the physical tile arrangement to collapse.
