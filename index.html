Flatness is the share of variance on the weakest principal axis (lower = flatter). “Flatter than” is the share of 2,000 random palettes of the same size that are less flat than yours. A neutral ramp plus one accent is nearly flat by construction, so compare against that baseline before reading meaning into a flat shape.
